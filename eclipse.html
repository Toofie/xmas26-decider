Before you go

Fishing intel, booze, traps

🎣📋

What's biting (Shoalhaven)

Crookhaven River: flathead, bream, whiting + summer tailor off the banks and river mouth; dusk + run-in. Culburra surf: salmon + tailor gutters dawn/dusk, metal slices (lifejackets on any rocks). Lake Wollumboola: fishing allowed (Jervis Bay NP — not a sanctuary), but the lake rarely opens to the sea; birdwalk unless it's just broken out, and no swimming (birds). Avoid Jervis Bay Marine Park no-take zones (Moona Moona, Hyams, Hare Bay, Callala…). Limits: flathead 36–70 slot/5, bream 25/10, whiting 27/20, tailor 30/10, mulloway 70/1.

🪱🛒

Bait + tackle

Tackle World Nowra (call ahead) · frozen fallback: Woolies Culburra + Nowra servos · fresh prawns: Jim Wild's + Nowra Fresh Fish & Meat Market (open 7 days incl holidays). Licence $7/3 days/adult, kids free.

🍾🛒

Booze + supplies

Berry IGA Plus Liquor 6am–9pm · Berry Bottle Shop 9–8:30 · BWS/Dan Murphy's Nowra · Heads Hotel bottle shop. Woolworths Culburra in town (call re PH hours) · Nowra Fresh for the big seafood run.

⚠️🕳️

Traps already dodged

Jim Wild's closed 26 Dec & 1 Jan — oysters go 27–28 Dec, ring 02 4447 1498 · Sun Sun banquet table — ring ahead · main beach rips → flags/SLSC end or Tilbury Cove · bull sharks: no dawn/dusk wading or swimming · dolphin cruise books out — book before you leave Auburn.

Rainy day + health

Hoyts Nowra (Boxing-day releases — prebook) · Shoalhaven Maritime Museum Huskisson if you're north anyway. ED: Shoalhaven Hospital, Nowra ~25 min (02 4423 9263).

🎣 Catch here

Crookhaven + Seven Mile ★★★★½ — verified best shore base

For Eclipse, Culburra Beach — river, surf and lake within walking distance; kid-friendly wharves at Crookhaven Heads, Orient Point + Greenwell Point (foreshore park). No tackle shop in Culburra — bait at Greenwell Point/Nowra (15–20 min). Tide window: last hour run-out → first hour run-in → top of tide.
Crookhaven River banks + Greenwell Point 4.5/5

"Just about every south-eastern estuary species" — trophy flathead + big mulloway, bream on the drop-offs, whiting in the shallows; summer tailor, luderick + crabs. Comerong Island breakwall for tailor/salmon/mulloway. Jim Wild's + Pelican Rocks sell fresh prawns that double as bait; pump your own nippers (bag 100).

Culburra surf beach + Seven Mile 3.5/5 — dawn

Whiting + bream in the gutters on dawn/dusk tides; salmon + tailor at first/last light off Penguin Head rocks (metal slices, lifejackets). High summer surf can be patchy.

Lake Wollumboola only when open

Fishing is allowed — it's Jervis Bay NP, not an aquatic reserve. But the lake rarely opens to the sea, so it only fishes after a entrance breakout; treat it as the birdwalk unless it's just opened.

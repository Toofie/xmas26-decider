Before you go

Fishing intel, booze, traps

🎣📋

What's biting (Brisbane Water ★★★★)

Flathead 36–70 cm slot (max 5), Dec–Apr peak, high tide, soft prawn plastics · bream 25 cm/10, dusk–night, peeled prawn · whiting 27 cm/20, dawn run-in, nippers · tailor 30 cm/10 at Ettalong "The Rip" dusk, pilchards (strong current — adults only) · mulloway 70 cm+ at night in the deep holes.

🎟️⚖️

Rules

$7/3-day licence/adult (bait + prawns covered), under-18s free. Bouddi's ocean side = no fishing. Never interfere with oyster leases — Brisbane Water is working oyster country.

🍾🛒

Booze + supplies

Dan Murphy's West Gosford (9am Boxing Day) · BWS Woy Woy · OBH drive-thru Umina. Fresh: Coles Kincumber (big shop), FoodWorks Empire Bay (top-ups), Woy Woy Wharf market prawns, Broken Bay Pearl Farm for the good oysters.

⚠️🕳️

Traps already dodged

Ferry day = Mekong bánh mì picnic · Ettalong = foreshore + ferry (not the markets) · Peking Garden/Hardy's Bay Club/Link & Pin closed Mon 28 · Edogawa + Saddles closed Boxing Day · mozzies at dusk · no wading at dawn/dusk (bull sharks) · ferry from Wagstaffe, not the door.

🏥🚑

Health

Gosford Hospital ED ~20 min. Kincumber + Woy Woy GP clusters. Pharmacies: Kincumber + Ettalong.

🎣 Catch here

Brisbane Water ★★★★

For Woy Woy Bay · Point Clare · Empire Bay — 18 jetties within 6 km of Point Clare
Your jetty / Woy Woy Bay foreshore 4/5 summer

Flathead + bream on the shallow margins, whiting on the run-in. Dawn + 2 hrs around high tide; bream at night on live prawns or pilchard.

🪱 Live prawns · nippers (pump your own, legal) · pilchards
Blackwall Rd foreshore flats 4/5 — peak flathead window

Dec–Apr is flathead prime time here. Soft prawn lures 100–140 mm or whitebait on the last of the run-in. Shallow grass foreshore — toddler can play while you fish.

Ettalong channel “The Rip” 3.5/5

Tailor at dusk on whole pilchards (Jan–Mar). Strong current — adults only, no kids near the edge.

Point Clare jetty 3.5/5 — night

Bream, whiting, small flathead under the lights after dark; tailor possible at dusk.

Dusky flathead36–70 cm · 5

⚠️ Bull sharks breed in these estuaries — no swimming or wading at dawn/dusk/night or after rain. Never step on oyster leases (active farms).
Line fishing fine everywhere here; nets/traps banned (landing net + bait trap OK). Bait gap: no dedicated tackle shop verified on the Woy Woy side — BCF West Gosford or the Wharf market for prawns, Woolies frozen as backup.

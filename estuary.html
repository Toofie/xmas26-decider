Before you go

Fishing intel, booze, traps

🎣📋

What's biting (St Georges Basin ★★★★★)

Basin View flats 5/5 on the doorstep: bream/flathead/whiting off sand + weed, dawn before the NE wind (~9–10am) + dusk · Sanctuary Point foreshore 5 min · Holden St ramp Vincentia whiting/tailor · Sussex Inlet channel mulloway/tailor run-out (strong current — adults only). Basin is NOT in Jervis Bay Marine Park — normal NSW rules. Species: flathead 36–70 cm slot/5, bream 25 cm/10, whiting 27 cm/20, tailor 30 cm/10, mulloway 70 cm/1. May 2026: 150k stocked mulloway + flathead into south-coast systems.

🚫⚖️

Sanctuary zones — NO fishing

Jervis Bay Marine Park no-take zones: Moona Moona Ck, Currambene Ck, Hare Bay, Callala, Hyams Beach, Wowly Gully, The Docks, Bowen Island + more. Huskisson Wharf = line fishing OK. Licence $7/3 days/adult (bait + yabby pumping covered), under-18 free.

🍾🛒

Booze + supplies

Basin View Cellars 2 min · BWS + Liquorland Vincentia 12 min · Dan Murphy's Nowra 9–9. Takeaway grog is UNRESTRICTED 26 + 28 Dec (NSW only bans Xmas + Good Friday). Fresh: IGA Basin View 2 min · Woolworths/Coles Vincentia 7–10 · Nowra Fresh Fish + Meat Market open 7 days incl holidays (~40 min, the big seafood run).

⚠️🕳️

Traps already dodged

No patrolled beach anywhere in Jervis Bay — toddler swims = Moona Moona Creek (shallow, calm, beside Huskisson's climbing playground) + the Basin foreshore itself; flags only at Cudmirrah/Sussex (daily 22 Dec–26 Jan) · Hyams village parking gridlocks Boxing week — park Greenfield Beach, walk in · Jim Wild's closed 26 Dec & 1 Jan — ring 02 4447 1498 · deep water at the jetty door: lifejacket + adult always · bull sharks in the Basin: no dawn/dusk wading.

🌧️🏥

Rainy day + health

Bay & Basin Leisure Centre, Vincentia (heated pools, 40 m slide, toddler pool, café, 10 min) · Jervis Bay Maritime Museum $14/kids under 16 FREE, 10–4 daily (Lady Denman ferry to board) · Huskisson Pictures historic cinema. ED: Shoalhaven Hospital Nowra ~25 min (02 4423 9263).

🎣 Catch here

St Georges Basin ★★★★★

For Estuary House, Basin View — the fishing monster of the shortlist. Basin View foreshore is ON YOUR DOORSTEP
Basin View foreshore + Lions Park flats 5/5 — zero drive

Bream, flathead, whiting off the sand and weed; gentle wading, grass reserve for the toddler. Dawn is the firing window — the NE breeze shuts the flats down by ~9–10am. 150,000 extra mulloway + flathead were stocked into south coast systems in May 2026.

🪱 Nippers (pump your own on the flats!) · prawns · ¼ oz soft plastics for flatties
Sussex Inlet channel 4/5 — the trophy spot

Mulloway, tailor, flathead on the run-out tide. Strong current — banked shoreline only.

Holden St ramp + Collingwood Beach, Vincentia 4/5

Jervis Bay side: whiting, bream, tailor; pump beach worms and pipis for bait.

⚠️ Marine park trap: the lake (St Georges Basin) is NOT in Jervis Bay Marine Park — normal rules. But the bay side has no-take sanctuary zones: Moona Moona Ck, Currambene Ck, Hare Bay, Callala, Hyams Beach — no fishing. Bull sharks in the basin channels; no fish-cleaning or swimming at dusk.
Bait + tackle on-site: Pelican Shores Marina, Basin View (5 min, confirmed bait & tackle). Backup: Sussex Tackle Shop (20 min) or Tackle World Nowra.

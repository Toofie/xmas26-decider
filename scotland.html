Before you go

Island logistics, fishing rules, contacts

🛒📦

Shopping: everything mainland-side

Woolworths Mona Vale (big shop) · Mona Vale Seafood (prawns, fish, bait) · BWS Newport (grog) · Church Point Bottle Shop (top-up). No shops, no deliveries to the island.

⛴️📞

Boats + parking

Church Point Ferry 0408 296 997 — hourly PH timetable, adult $12.30, kids under 5 free, last mainland departure ~6:30pm. Pink Water Taxi 0428 238 190 (8am–8pm PH) ~$46, surcharged 26 & 28 Dec, EFTPOS. Parking: marina first, Bakers Rd free backup (~$40/day peak at the marina).

🎟️🎣

Fishing licence + limits

$7 per adult / 3 days — buy online in 2 min before you drive. Kids under 18 free; an adult helping a kid doesn't need one. Bream/whiting/flathead standard bag limits (20 bream+tarwhine, 20 whiting, 5 dusky flathead min 36 cm). Pittwater is ★★★★½ fishing: bream, whiting, flathead, squid, tailor; kingfish off West Head in summer.

🦈⚠️

Water safety

Deep water at the jetty door, no pool, unfenced yard edges, 3 stair levels. Lifejacket + adult for Oscar at the water, always. No dawn/dusk swimming — bull sharks are in Pittwater year-round.

🏥🚑

Health

Nearest ED: Northern Beaches Hospital, Frenchs Forest (~25–30 min incl. ferry + car). Mona Vale has a GP urgent-care cluster on the stroller strip.

🎣 Catch here

Pittwater ★★★★½

For Scotland Island — fish straight off the island jetties, or the mainland spots
Palm Beach public wharf 4.5/5 — fish this first

Railed and pram-friendly. Bream + flathead year-round, squid over the ribbon weed on the rising tide, whiting on the summer sand flats — and bonito run Dec–Apr with kingfish often sitting under them. First and last light.

🦑 Fresh jigged squid is THE local bait · live prawns/nippers cover the rest
Scotland Island jetties (Carols + Eastern wharves) 3.5/5

Bream + flathead at dawn/dusk around the piles, squid on the weed edges — a 2-minute walk from the house. Local boats chase metre-plus kings around the moorings (boat territory).

Winnererremy Bay, Mona Vale 3/5 — best toddler station

Flying Fox playground right beside shallow bream/whiting flats. Fish while the 3-year-old plays.

Bayview Baths + Church Point flats 3/5

Calmest water in the system, bream/flathead prospecting at sunrise. Baths are fully enclosed — toddler swim heaven.

⚠️ Bull sharks live in warm summer Pittwater (tagged fish + sightings) — swim with Oscar away from dawn/dusk. Lifejackets mandatory on any ocean rocks.
No aquatic reserve inside Pittwater — line fishing fine. Net ban only east of Barrenjoey (caulerpa closure; rods untouched). Tackle: Narrabeen Bait & Tackle, 1469 Pittwater Rd, opens 7am weekends.

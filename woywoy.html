Before you go

Fishing intel, booze, traps

🎣📋

What's biting (Brisbane Water ★★★★)

Dusky flathead 36–70 cm slot, max 5 — Dec–Apr peak, sandflats + drains, high tide, soft prawn 100–140 mm · Bream 25 cm/10 — best at dusk–night, peeled prawn, berley the pylons · Sand whiting 27 cm/20 — Nov–Mar, dawn + rising tide, worms/nippers · Tailor 30 cm/10 — Jan–Mar dusk, whole pilchard, Ettalong channel ("The Rip", strong current, adults only) · Mulloway 70 cm+, 1/person — night, deep holes, fresh squid.

🎟️⚖️

Rules

$7/3-day licence per adult (covers bait + prawn collecting), under-18s free, adult helping a kid exempt. Max 4 rods, 3 hooks/line. Bouddi's ocean side = no fishing. Never touch oyster leases.

🍾🛒

Booze + supplies

Dan Murphy's West Gosford (9am Boxing Day) · BWS Woy Woy Deepwater Plaza · Ocean Beach Hotel drive-thru (10am–late). Bottle shops trade on Boxing Day — NSW restricted days are Xmas + Good Friday only. Fresh: Woolies Woy Woy + Umina, Wharf market prawns, Broken Bay Pearl Farm (premium).

⚠️🕳️

Traps already dodged

Fishermen's Wharf closed Wednesdays (Wed 30 = dead → oyster-farm lunch instead) · Peking Garden + Link & Pin + Hardy's Bay Club closed Mon 28 · Edogawa Garden + Saddles closed Boxing Day · no wading at dawn/dusk (bull sharks) · mozzies at dusk — repellent before dark.

🏥🚑

Health

Gosford Hospital ED 15–20 min from Woy Woy Bay. GP clusters in Woy Woy + Umina.

🎣 Catch here

Brisbane Water ★★★★

For Woy Woy Bay · Point Clare — 18 jetties within 6 km of Point Clare
Your jetty / Woy Woy Bay foreshore 4/5 summer

Flathead + bream on the shallow margins, whiting on the run-in. Dawn + 2 hrs around high tide; bream at night on live prawns or pilchard.

🪱 Live prawns · nippers (pump your own, legal) · pilchards
Blackwall Rd foreshore flats 4/5 — peak flathead window

Dec–Apr is flathead prime time here. Soft prawn lures 100–140 mm or whitebait on the last of the run-in. Shallow grass foreshore — toddler can play while you fish.

Ettalong channel “The Rip” 3.5/5

Tailor at dusk on whole pilchards (Jan–Mar). Strong current — adults only, no kids near the edge.

Point Clare jetty 3.5/5 — night

Bream, whiting, small flathead under the lights after dark; tailor possible at dusk.

Dusky flathead36–70 cm · 5

⚠️ Bull sharks breed in these estuaries — no swimming or wading at dawn/dusk/night or after rain. Never step on oyster leases (active farms).
Line fishing fine everywhere here; nets/traps banned (landing net + bait trap OK). Bait gap: no dedicated tackle shop verified on the Woy Woy side — BCF West Gosford or the Wharf market for prawns, Woolies frozen as backup.
